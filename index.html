About me

I make people feel a part of something bigger than themselves.

Whether it's a brand launching something new, a guest walking through the door or the team making it all happen, it's the micro-moments that matter most. First impressions form in seconds. A warm welcome, a name remembered, a problem solved before anyone noticed: these small moments shape how people feel long after the experience is over.

I've spent the last few years creating those moments. I've staffed 75+ festival activations across the country, run the front door at a 400-cover restaurant and delivered five-star service on a private yacht off Rhode Island. The setting changes, but the job is the same: get the details right, so the people involved feel seen, heard and part of something.

Let's work together to make your brand stand out and create moments people remember. Let's find people who want to be there, who bring the energy and share your vision.
